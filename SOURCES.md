### Franklin's Way to Wealth; or, "Poor Richard Improved"
- **Author:** Benjamin Franklin
- **Project Gutenberg EBook #:** 43855
- **URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/43855
- **Plain-text file:** https://www.gutenberg.org/files/43855/43855-0.txt
